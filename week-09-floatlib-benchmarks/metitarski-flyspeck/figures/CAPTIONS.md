# MetiTarski and Flyspeck with interval: figure captions

Written by `scripts/plot.py`; do not edit by hand. Every figure is `NAME.png` (9 inches wide at 200 dpi) and `NAME.svg`. An image holds only the plot, its axes with units, a legend with short series names and panel letters; the caption says what the figure shows, what was measured, in which unit, on which scales, where and when. The notes say how, which numbers come from Lean and which from scripts, and give the numbers behind the marks.

**Figure 1.** What FloatLib's interval tactic proves on the MetiTarski problems and on Flyspeck's nonlinear inequalities. A bar is one route over a whole suite, split into the goals proved, not proved and not translated to Lean, with the number proved at its end: 948 MetiTarski goals and 164 Flyspeck records, one tactic call each. Run on 2026-11-20 with Lean 4.36.0 on an Intel Xeon Platinum 8275CL (96 threads, shared with other jobs); these are the suite runs recorded in the repository of FloatLib 4.1.0.

Alt text: Four horizontal bars on an axis from 0 to 100 percent, for MetiTarski and Flyspeck with interval alone and with the constraint engine. The blue part, the proved goals, is largest, about a third, for MetiTarski with the constraint engine; the Flyspeck bars are mostly hatched, not translated. Each bar ends in its count.

Notes: Each goal is translated to one Lean goal and given to `interval` once with `FloatLib.Tactic.Interval` alone and once with the constraint engine imported as well, which is `interval` as `import FloatLib` provides it; 20 seconds and 4 GB per goal. MetiTarski: 124 and 340 of 948; Flyspeck: 3 and 6 of 164 (153 nonlinear inequalities and eleven examples). Not proved means refused or over the limits; not translated means the translator refuses the goal. A count says what the tactic did on these suites, not what it does on all goals. From Lean: every proof and its axioms. From scripts: the translation and the counts. The counts are `benchmarks/results/suites/counts.csv` of FloatLib's repository (inequalities 2026-11-20T11:44:50Z to 2026-11-20T12:03:31Z); the library sources of that run differ from the 4.1.0 tag by one added corollary and the version number, and no tactic differs. Source: `results/counts.csv`.
