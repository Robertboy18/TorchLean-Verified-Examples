# LLM2Cert's obligations with interval: figure captions

Written by `scripts/plot.py`; do not edit by hand. Every figure is `NAME.png` (9 inches wide at 200 dpi) and `NAME.svg`. An image holds only the plot, its axes with units, a legend with short series names and panel letters; the caption says what the figure shows, what was measured, in which unit, on which scales, where and when. **Figure 1.** The 291 proof obligations of LLM2Cert's benchmark DynaCertBench, each given to FloatLib's interval tactic as the one-line proof `by interval` with no model, next to LLM2Cert's own All-Lean result, in which a language model writes the proofs. Panel (a) counts the obligations that Lean accepts with the three standard axioms only, panel (b) the 81 certificates all of whose obligations are closed, and panel (c) is the time Lean ran, in hours on a linear axis: for interval the sum over all 291 files, for LLM2Cert the time on the obligations it closed, to which its 1.94 million model tokens are added. interval was run on FloatLib 4.1.0 on 2026-11-20 with Lean 4.36.0 on an Intel Xeon Platinum 8275CL (96 threads, shared with other jobs); LLM2Cert's numbers are those of its published records.

Alt text: Three panels of three horizontal bars each, two blue bars for interval with Lean's default limits and with raised limits and one grey bar for LLM2Cert. In the first two panels the three bars are of similar length, each ending in its count; in the third the two blue bars are a small fraction of the grey bar, and each bar ends in its time.

Notes: `interval` alone closes 281 of 291 obligations and completes 71 of 81 certificates with Lean's default `maxHeartbeats` and `maxRecDepth` (43.8 min of Lean time over all 291 files, median 3.8 s per closed obligation), and 282 and 72 with the limits raised to 4,000,000 heartbeats and a recursion depth of 100,000 (49.5 min, median 3.9 s); no model and no tokens. LLM2Cert All-Lean: 274 of 291, 64 of 81, 13.6 h on the obligations it closed and 1.94 million tokens. Against LLM2Cert All-Lean (274): with the default limits 266 obligations are closed by both and 15 only by `interval`; with the raised limits 267 and 15. Six Lean processes at a time, 600 seconds per obligation; the slowest closed obligation took 488.7 s and 470.6 s on the shared machine, and with 300 seconds the counts are 280 and 70 with the default limits, 281 and 71 with the raised ones. Every statement is LLM2Cert's, verbatim; only the proof is generated, and an obligation counts as closed only when Lean accepts the file and `#print axioms` shows exactly `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. Three of the 291 statements say that a function is radially unbounded, which is no set of constraints for `interval` to refute. From Lean: whether each obligation closed. From scripts: the files, the wall time of each Lean process, the counts and the sums; LLM2Cert's numbers (their `lean_records.json`). Source: `results/llm2cert.csv`.
